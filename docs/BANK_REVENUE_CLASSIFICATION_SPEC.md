# Bank Statement Revenue Classification and Period Normalisation

Status: Phases 1 and 2 implemented as **pure modules, not wired into scoring, the parser, the database or the API**. The scoring formula and the existing bank-statement override are unchanged.

## 1. Problem (from docs/REVENUE_SCALE_COMPARISON.md)
1. The current parser reads a pre-labelled `parsed_revenue` figure; nothing says what it contains (sales vs loans, transfers, owner money).
2. The period covered by that figure is undefined.
3. An approved statement overrides declared revenue although approval of a document is not verification of its contents.

## 2. Phase 1: deposit classification (`services/deposit_classifier.py`)
Each credit is classified before any revenue is calculated.

| Category | Treatment |
|---|---|
| customer_sale | Included as revenue |
| loan | Excluded |
| owner_contribution | Excluded |
| internal_transfer | Excluded |
| refund_reversal | Excluded |
| unknown | Excluded, flagged `needs_review` |

Per transaction the record keeps: date, description, amount, category, confidence, source (`rule:<id>` / `conflict` / `default` / `manual:<reviewer>`), review status (`auto` / `needs_review` / `reviewed`).

Rules (`rules-v1`, ordered keyword rules, no ML):
- A deposit counts as revenue only when classified as a customer sale with confidence of at least 0.70 and not awaiting review.
- **Unknown never becomes revenue.** No matching rule means unknown plus review.
- A description matching both a sale rule and a non-sale rule (for example "LOAN REPAYMENT INVOICE") is a **conflict**: unknown plus review, never guessed.
- Several non-sale matches take a fixed most-specific precedence (owner, transfer, loan, refund); all are excluded anyway.
- A person's decision (`apply_review`) overrides the rules, requires a reviewer name and is recorded as `manual:<name>`.
- Debits and zero amounts are not deposits and are ignored.

Known limits of keyword rules (to be measured on real statements, not assumed solved): descriptions are free text and vary by bank; a customer whose name contains a keyword (for example "OWNER") will be sent to review rather than counted; wording such as "SALE OF EQUIPMENT" can match a sale rule. Rule-based first, ML only if labelled review decisions later justify it.

## 3. Phase 2: period normalisation (`services/revenue_normalisation.py`)
Input: classified deposits plus the statement's actual start and end dates. Output (`RevenueObservation`) keeps separate, never merged:

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| total_deposits | every credit in the period |
| observed_customer_revenue | classified customer sales (rules and/or review) |
| reviewed_customer_revenue | the part a person confirmed |
| excluded_by_category | loans, owner contributions, transfers, refunds |
| unresolved_deposits | unknown or conflicting, excluded |
| months_covered, monthly_average | actual coverage, observed / months |
| annualised_run_rate | an **estimate** (observed x 12 / months), never actual annual revenue |
| basis | `observed_12_months`, `run_rate` or `not_reliable` |

An annual estimate is withheld (`not_reliable`) when the period is under 3 months or more than 25% of deposit value is unresolved. Transactions outside the stated period are ignored and counted. A period of about 12 months (11.5 to 12.5) is reported as observed, not as a run rate. Worked example: 1 Jan to 30 Apr, verified customer deposits R50,000, 4 months, monthly average R12,500, run rate R150,000.

## 4. Target evidence levels (design intent; not yet implemented)
The engine should eventually distinguish, instead of one `revenue` number:
1. Declared revenue (founder statement).
2. Bank-derived observed revenue (all deposits in the period).
3. Verified customer revenue (classified sales, review complete).
4. Estimated annualised revenue (run rate, labelled as an estimate).

Approval of a document and verification of its financial contents are separate states. Until levels 3 and 4 exist in the engine, the current override stays as is and this is recorded as a known gap.

## 5. Transaction extractor (synthetic only; `services/transaction_extractor.py`)
Standalone and **unwired** (a test fails if production code imports it). Built and tested only on fictional statements generated by `backend/testing/make_synthetic_statement.py` (fixtures in `backend/tests/fixtures`).

Behaviour: reads a text-layer PDF, finds the column header on each page, extracts date, description (wrapped lines joined with one space), debit, credit and balance, reads the statement period and opening/closing balances, and hands credits to the deposit classifier and then the period normaliser. **Nothing is dropped silently**: unreadable rows become failures with page, text and reason. A statement is `trustworthy` only with no failures, opening + credits - debits = closing, and an unbroken running balance on every row. Untrustworthy statements yield no revenue observation.

Synthetic results (25 rows, 19 deposits):

| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| Rows extracted | 25 of 25, 0 failures |
| Dates, amounts, direction, balances, descriptions | exact match to ground truth |
| Identical same-day rows, repeats, month-end span, wrapped description | all kept |
| Reconciliation | opening R10,000 + credits - debits = closing, difference 0.00 |
| Statement with one row removed | detected: difference R7,500, running balance breaks at the gap |
| Statement with one misread amount | reported as 1 failure; 24 rows + 1 failure = 25, not silently dropped |
| Non-PDF / truncated / empty input | reported as failures, no exception |

Classifier quality on the same set, measured separately from extraction: 11 genuine sales, 10 found, 1 missed, 1 false positive (precision 10/11, recall 10/11). Revenue is overstated by R5,300 (R46,650 against R41,350). The two errors are the ones predicted: `SALE OF EQUIPMENT` counted as revenue (generic SALE keyword) and `OWNER STORES PAYMENT` excluded as an owner contribution. Both are strict xfails. `CUSTOMER REFUND`, `LOAN REPAYMENT INVOICE` and an unmatched cash deposit go to review and are not revenue.

Extraction limitations: text-layer PDFs only (no scanned or image statements, no OCR); one layout (header row with right-aligned Debit/Credit/Balance columns, dates `DD Mon YYYY`, ZAR-style `1,234.56`); descriptions that run into the amount columns are not supported; multi-account or multi-currency statements untested; anything else is reported as a failure rather than guessed. Because it was designed against its own synthetic layout, passing these tests says nothing yet about real bank layouts. Real evaluation needs a labelled set across several banks, with extraction accuracy and classification precision/recall reported separately, using redacted statements and permission.

## 5a. Rules v2 (conservative fix of the two demonstrated errors)
Changes, none of which add broad keywords:
- A bare `SALE`/`SALES` word no longer establishes customer revenue. Revenue needs a payment processor, an invoice reference, or the weaker "customer / payment from" pattern. `SALE OF EQUIPMENT` now goes to review and is not revenue.
- A bare `OWNER`/`DIRECTOR`/`SHAREHOLDER`/`MEMBER` word no longer excludes a deposit. Owner money needs a paired phrase (for example `OWNER CAPITAL`, `DIRECTOR LOAN`, `CAPITAL INJECTION`). A bare owner word sends the deposit to review (`ambiguous:owner-word`), unless a strong sale signal (invoice reference or payment processor) resolves it: `INV 20491 OWNER STORES` is revenue; `PAYMENT FROM OWNER STORES` and `OWNER'S MARKET PAYMENT` are review.
- Everything else is unchanged: loans, owner capital, transfers and refunds stay excluded; conflicts, unknowns and unmatched cash deposits stay in review.

Synthetic result, rules-v2: 11 genuine sales, 10 counted, 1 held for review, **0 false positives** (precision 10/10, recall 10/11). Observed customer revenue R39,150 against a true R41,350: **understated by R2,200** (the held-for-review `OWNER STORES PAYMENT`) instead of overstated by R5,300. Recall was deliberately not bought back with looser rules. The two former strict xfails are now passing tests, and `tests/behavioral/test_deposit_classifier_rules.py` pins the rule table.

**Rules are frozen** until a labelled, anonymised evaluation set from several banks exists. These synthetic results show the pipeline works on its designed layout; they do not establish real-world accuracy.

**Open policy decision (explicit, undecided): refunds.** Credits classified as refunds are excluded from revenue and reported separately. Customer refunds paid out are debits, which the classifier ignores, so they do not yet reduce revenue. Options: net them as a negative adjustment to customer revenue, or report them separately. Until decided, observed revenue can be overstated by refunds paid to customers.
